Net PP&E, depreciation, capital expenditure

The two-stage example: sales of 32.00 in year 1, net PP&E held at 50% of next year’s sales.

Depreciation rate d 10%
Depreciationt = d × net PP&Et−1
Growth scale 1.00
Sales growth 25.0% → 15.0% → 8.0%

(a) Net PP&E

Net PP&Et = 0.50 × salest+1

(b) Change in net PP&E

Net PP&Et − net PP&Et−1

(c) Depreciation

d × opening net PP&E

(d) Capital expenditure

change in net PP&E + depreciation

Panels (b), (c) and (d) share one vertical scale, so panel (d) is panel (b) stacked on panel (c), bar by bar.

The depreciation rate leaves panels (a) and (b) alone: the asset base is pinned to next year’s sales whatever d is. It moves depreciation and capital expenditure together, one for one.

In the steady state the asset base grows 8.0% a year, so capital expenditure settles at 1.80 times depreciation.
